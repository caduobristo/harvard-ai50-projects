# Analysis

## Layer 5, Head 8

This attention head appears to capture the relationship between determiners and the nouns they modify. When examining the attention patterns, determiners like "the" and "a" show strong attention to the main noun in their noun phrase. This suggests the model learns to associate articles with their corresponding nouns as a fundamental syntactic relationship.

Example Sentences:
- "The little cat sat on the [MASK]."
- "He drank a cup of [MASK] in the morning."

## Layer 6, Head 7

This attention head seems to focus on the relationship between prepositions and their objects. Prepositions such as "of", "on", "to", and "before" display high attention scores toward the nouns that complete the prepositional phrase. This indicates the model captures how prepositions connect to their objects to form meaningful phrases.

Example Sentences:
- "She walked to the [MASK] before sunset."
- "The little cat sat on the [MASK]."
